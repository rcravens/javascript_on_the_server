Scaling Up – Beyond the Basics

Why Use A Framework?

  • Vanilla Node is flexible but requires manual routing, middleware, and error handling.
  • Frameworks provide:
    • 👉 Structure (folders, conventions).
    • 👉 Built-in features (middleware, validation, authentication).
    • 👉 Performance optimizations.
    • 👉 Community support.
